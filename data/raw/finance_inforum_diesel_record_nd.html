Diesel hits record price in North Dakota

AAA reports North Dakota’s average diesel price reached $6.13 per gallon on Thursday, Sept. 17.

By Josh Mathisen
September 17, 2026 at 6:43 PM

FARGO — It was another record for diesel fuel in North Dakota on Thursday, Sept. 17.

AAA reports the average diesel price hit $6.13 in the state. That comes as farmers are in the heart of harvest season.

ADVERTISEMENT

Diesel prices are up nearly a full dollar per gallon compared to last month and $2.50 a gallon from last year.

Farmers are feeling the squeeze with high input costs across the board, including fertilizer and impacts of the drought.

They're hoping strong yields and high commodity prices will help make up some of the difference.

One farmer says he tried to get ahead of the price surge.

"I saw with the conflict in Iran that I thought that there could be some potentiality and possibly not being able to get the diesel that we need to facilitate harvest," said Jacob Greer, a soybean and corn farmer attending Big Iron in Fargo. "So, I figured it was cheaper to have it in the tank, even if it was a high price, than to not have it at all."

Another farmer at Big Iron said the rising price of diesel will cost him an estimated $40,000 this year.
