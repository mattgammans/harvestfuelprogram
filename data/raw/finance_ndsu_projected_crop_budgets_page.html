Projected Crop Budgets

Text

North Dakota regional crop budgets are created annually and we have provided the current as well as past issues here.

Sections

PROJECTED CROP BUDGETS

Crop budgets are prepared by region.

North West: Burke, Divide, McKenzie, Mountrail, and Williams counties.
North Central: Benson, Bottineau, McHenry, McLean, Pierce, Renville, Rolette, and Ward counties.
North East: Cavalier, Nelson, Ramsey and Towner counties.
North Valley: Grand Forks, Pembina, and Walsh counties.
South West: Adams, Billings, Bowman, Dunn, Golden Valley, Grant, Hettinger, Mercer, Morton, Oliver, Sioux, Slope, and Stark counties.
South Central: Burleigh, Emmons, Kidder, Logan, McIntosh, and Sheridan counties.
East Central: Eddy, Foster, Griggs, Stutsman, and Wells counties.
South East: Barnes, Dickey, LaMoure, Ransom, Sargent, and Steele counties.
South Valley: Cass, Richland, and Traill counties.
