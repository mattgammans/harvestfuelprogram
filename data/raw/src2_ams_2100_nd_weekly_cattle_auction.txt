Only 1 reported auction this week, no reported auctions a week ago to offer a comparison. Very good demand for all weights of steers and
heifers, however the best demand remains centered on light cattle to go to grass, and for replacement quality heifers. Lower cash fed cattle
market, 258.00-261.00, and a CME cattle futures complex falling sharply has cattle feeders feeling more apprehensive about the road ahead.
Supply included: 100% Feeder Cattle (40% Steers, 60% Heifers). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 60%.
