Farm Machinery Management

Download the Farm Machinery Economic Cost Estimation Spreadsheet by clicking MACHDATA.xlsm (contains macros.  To make the macros operate properly, you will probably need to save the spreadsheet to your own computer and then re-open it from there.  Then, if prompted click the "Enable content" button.  Requires Excel 2007 or later.   The accompanying paper is Machinery Cost Estimates (pdf), updated in June, 2024.
