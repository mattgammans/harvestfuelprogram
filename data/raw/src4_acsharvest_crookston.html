Ticket Correction Policy and Deadline

It shall be company policy that American Crystal Sugar Company shareholders are allowed delivery ticket transfers and corrections to be made through the 7th day following the date of station closure to which the delivery was made or October 31, whichever occurs first. Delivery ticket transfer refers to any delivery that is transferred from one contract to another. A service fee of $10 per delivery ticket transfer will apply to all transfers that exceed 3% of a grower unit’s total deliveries, and no delivery tickets will be allowed to be transferred more than one time.

Stay Pay Guidelines

Stay Pay Guidelines
1. Employee will be paid a minimum of 4hrs of pay for any weather event shutdown
2. If the shutdown occurs on M-F of the week, it will be paid as regular time(1x). If it occurs on a Sat-Sun, if will be paid at OT (1.5x) rate.
3. Employee will have to report to work on their next scheduled shift and punch in when their receiving station reopens in order to receive Stay Pay.
4. Stay pay will be paid out in the same pay period the shutdowns occur in.
5. If the weather event shutdown overlaps the pay period end, they will be paid stay pay. If they do not report to work when their site reopens, they will be subject to losing their bonus.
6. The 4hr minimum for stay pay only applies to stockpile harvest. During prepile, we will pay the normal 2hrs of show up pay if we haven’t updated the harvest hotline in time and employees report to work.
7. Employees will have to call the harvest hotline prior to their shift as always. If the hotline says to report to work, they will need to report to work in order to receive stay pay.
8. Any other situation is at full discretion of the ag staff.

Pop Up Blockers and Shareholder Records

If you have pop up blockers loaded on your computer and you are having trouble accessing your shareholder information, please go into the Internet on your computer and do the following:

  1. Click on Tools from the Toolbar at the top of your page
  2. Click on Internet Options (usually the last item to choose on the list)
  3. Click on the Security Tab
  4. Click on Trusted Sites
  5. Click on Sites and add https://web400.crystalsugar.com
  6. Click on Okay
  7. Click on Tools once again
  8. Click on Pop Up Blocker Settings
  9. Click on Add to allow web400.crystalsugar.com
