Gov. Armstrong declares diesel fuel emergency amid high costs at pump

By Joe Skurzewski
Published: Sep. 29, 2026 at 4:52 PM CDT
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

BISMARCK, N.D. (KMOT) – Amid ongoing price hikes of diesel fuel, Gov. Kelly Armstrong, R-N.D., issued an executive order on Tuesday declaring a diesel emergency.

The declaration aims to provide temporary relief for farmers by expanding permitted uses of red-dyed diesel fuel to allow for on-highway use.

The order expands the use of dyed diesel fuel to licensed motor vehicles used in connection with ag operations, with the goal of opening up more affordable fuel for farmers for both in-field and on-highway work, in the midst of harvest season.

The order takes effect immediately and runs through the end of November. It does not affect the federal government’s 24.4-cent-per-gallon excise tax on diesel fuel.
