North Dakota Grain Industry Data (Grain Trax)

In cooperation with the North Dakota Department of Agriculture, the information used in the reports below is compiled from approximately 300 North Dakota elevators. For more information, please email kimberly.vachal@ndsu.edu or call (701)231-6425.

Monthly Grain & Oilseed Reports

Updated: August 21, 2026

The following reports will be updated after each month end.

  • ND Graphs (PDF, 424 KB) – These graphs show monthly rail shipments by railroad, by commodity (HRS, Durum, Barley, Sunflowers, and Corn), total truck shipments, total rail shipments, and all grain & oilseed shipments.
  • ND Tables (PDF, 292 KB) – These tables show market shares, modal shares, and destinations of grain & oilseed shipments originating from North Dakota elevators.
